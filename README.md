<h2>4. Device data processed on your headset</h2>
<p>
  Spatial Piano is a mixed-reality application. In order to work at all, it uses the following device
  capabilities. All of this is processed locally on your Meta Quest headset in real time and is discarded
  as soon as it has been used to render the current frame.
</p>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Hand tracking data.</strong> The position and pose of your hands and individual fingers, used
      to detect which holographic piano keys you are pressing. Spatial Piano is played with your hands
      only. We do not record, store, transmit or retain hand tracking data.</li>
  <li><strong>Passthrough and spatial data.</strong> The headset uses its cameras to place the holographic
      piano in your real room. The application receives only the resulting placement and surface
      information from the Meta Horizon OS. Nexlium does not receive, store or transmit raw camera images
      or video of your surroundings.</li>
  <li><strong>Audio output.</strong> The application generates piano audio locally. Spatial Piano does not
      request or use microphone access.</li>
</ul>

<h2>5. Data stored locally by the application</h2>
<p>
  The following is written to the application's private storage area on your Quest device so your progress
  is not lost between sessions:
</p>
<ul>
  <li>Lesson completion state and practice scores</li>
  <li>Song history and unlocked content flags</li>
  <li>Application settings such as piano placement, key size, volume and visual preferences</li>
</ul>
<p>
  This data stays on your device. It is deleted when you uninstall the application or clear its data from
  the Meta Quest system settings.
</p>

<h2>6. Data we do not collect</h2>
<p>To be explicit, Nexlium does not collect, receive or store:</p>
<ul>
  <li>Your name, email address, postal address or phone number</li>
  <li>Payment card details, billing information or transaction records</li>
  <li>Microphone audio or voice recordings</li>
  <li>Camera images, video or photographs of your environment</li>
  <li>Precise location data</li>
  <li>Biometric identifiers used to identify you as an individual</li>
  <li>Your Meta friends, followers or social graph</li>
</ul>

<h2>7. Purposes of processing</h2>
<p>We process the data described above only to:</p>
<ul>
  <li>Run, support and maintain the features of Spatial Piano that you have asked to use</li>
  <li>Save and restore your learning progress</li>
  <li>Verify entitlements and deliver content you have purchased</li>
  <li>Provide an age-appropriate experience and meet Meta's platform requirements</li>
  <li>Diagnose crashes and technical faults, using aggregated and anonymised information only</li>
</ul>
<p>
  We do not use your data for advertising, for profiling, for building marketing lists, or for any purpose
  unrelated to operating the application. We do not sell user data, and we do not share user data with data
  brokers.
</p>

<h2>8. Sharing with others</h2>
<p>
  Nexlium does not share your user data with third parties, advertisers, analytics vendors or data
  processors. The only party involved is Meta Platforms, which operates the Meta Horizon Store, provides the
  Platform SDK, and processes all payments for in-app purchases. Meta's handling of your data is governed by
  the <a href="https://www.meta.com/legal/privacy-policy/">Meta Privacy Policy</a> and the
  <a href="https://www.meta.com/legal/quest/supplemental-privacy-policy/">Supplemental Meta Platforms
  Technologies Privacy Policy</a>.
</p>
<p>
  We may disclose information where we are legally required to do so by a valid legal process. We have not
  provided personal data to public authorities in response to national security requests.
</p>

<h2>9. Retention</h2>
<p>
  Meta user data received through the Platform SDK is held in memory only for the duration of your session
  and is not retained afterwards. Locally stored progress and settings are retained on your device until you
  delete them, reset them in the app, or uninstall the application. Any support correspondence you send us
  by email is kept for up to 24 months and then deleted.
</p>

<h2>10. How to delete your data</h2>
<p>You can delete data associated with Spatial Piano in any of these ways:</p>
<ul>
  <li><strong>In the app.</strong> Open Settings and choose Reset Progress. This clears your lesson
      progress, song history and stored preferences from the device.</li>
  <li><strong>On the device.</strong> Uninstall Spatial Piano from your Meta Quest, or go to Settings &rarr;
      Apps &rarr; Spatial Piano &rarr; Clear Data. This removes all locally stored application data.</li>
  <li><strong>By email.</strong> Write to <a href="mailto:info@nexlium.com">info@nexlium.com</a> with
      the subject line "Data Deletion Request". We will confirm receipt within 5 business days and complete
      the request within 30 days. You do not need an account with us to make this request.</li>
</ul>
<p>
  You can also revoke the application's access to your Meta data at any time from the Meta Horizon mobile
  app or from your Quest device settings. To delete data held by Meta itself, use the controls in your Meta
  account.
</p>

<p>

  respond within 30 days.

<h2>12. Children</h2>
<p>
  Spatial Piano is not directed at children under 10, and users under 10 are not permitted on the Meta

  knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal
  information, contact <a href="mailto:info@nexlium.com">info@nexlium.com</a> and we will delete it.
</p>

<h2>13. Security</h2>
<p>
  Because Spatial Piano processes user data on your device rather than on our servers, the attack surface is
  deliberately small. Application data is stored in the app's private sandbox on the Quest, protected by the
  operating system. All communication with Meta's platform services uses encrypted connections provided by
  the Meta Platform SDK. Access to our developer accounts is restricted to authorised Nexlium personnel and
  protected by multi-factor authentication.
</p>

<h2>14. International transfers</h2>
<p>
  Nexlium is based in Pakistan. Because user data remains on your device and is not transmitted to us, we do
  not carry out international transfers of your personal data. Any data you send us by email is received in
  Pakistan.
</p>
